# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in AI Skill Library to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about technical problems and generous about people.
- Critique the skill, not the contributor.
- Assume a licence or safety question is asked in good faith, and answer it with evidence.
- Accept that a skill may be rejected. Rejection is about fit with `CATEGORY_INDEX.md`
  and `CONTRIBUTING.md` standards, not about the author.
- Give credit accurately. Never present someone else's work as your own.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Submitting skills that exfiltrate data, disable safety controls, hide instructions
  from the user, or execute unreviewed remote code.
- Submitting content whose licence does not permit redistribution.
- Sustained disruption of review threads.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone is
representing the project.

## Reporting

Report concerns to the maintainer listed in the repository profile. Reports are handled
privately. The maintainer will acknowledge within seven days, explain the next step, and
avoid involving the reported person in the handling of the report.

## Enforcement

| Severity | Response |
|----------|----------|
| Correction needed | Private note explaining the problem and what to change |
| Repeated or clear violation | Warning, with a required change in behaviour |
| Serious violation (harassment, doxxing, malicious skill) | Temporary or permanent ban from participation |

Decisions are recorded without personal detail in the project's private maintainer notes.
