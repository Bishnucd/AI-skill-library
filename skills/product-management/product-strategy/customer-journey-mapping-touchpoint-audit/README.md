# Customer Journey Mapping Touchpoint Audit

## Description
Maps end-to-end customer workflows across awareness, evaluation, onboarding, and renewal touchpoints. Use when uncovering experience bottlenecks and cross-channel friction points.

## Purpose
Customer Journey Mapping visualizes the complete process a customer goes through to accomplish a goal, plotting actions, emotional highs/lows, digital touchpoints, and operational backstage systems.

## When to Use
- When executing customer journey mapping touchpoint audit in production workflows.
- Use when structured standards, deterministic outputs, and error handling are required.

## When NOT to Use
- Ad-hoc, unstructured exploratory tasks where formal workflows add unnecessary overhead.
- Environments where required dependencies (Customer Journey Map, Touchpoint Audit) cannot be provisioned.

## Supported Technologies
- Customer Journey Map
- Touchpoint Audit
- UX Research
- Service Blueprint

## Example Use Cases
### E-Commerce Checkout Drop-Off Remediation
Discovering that surprise shipping calculations at the final payment step caused 40% cart abandonment.

### Healthcare Patient Portal Onboarding Audit
Streamlining multi-factor identity verification steps to prevent elderly patients from abandoning the portal.
