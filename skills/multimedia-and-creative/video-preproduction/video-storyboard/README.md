# video-storyboard

## What it does

Converts a script or brief into a shootable storyboard: beats, timed shots with framing and
action, audio and on-screen text, a shot list grouped by lighting setup, an asset checklist, and
an explicit list of risky shots with alternatives.

## Why it exists

Videos go over budget and over runtime because the plan is a script with no visual decisions in
it. Someone discovers on the shoot day that the key shot needs a lens nobody brought, and the edit
discovers that 90 seconds of footage was planned for a 60-second cut. A timed board with
feasibility checks surfaces both before anyone travels.

## When to use

- Planning a product, explainer, tutorial, or social video.
- Handing a plan to another person or an AI video tool.
- A script exists but the visuals are undefined.
- Scope keeps growing during editing.

## When not to use

- A single continuous recording with no cuts.
- Live-streamed or unscripted content.
- Feature-length narrative work, which needs a full production breakdown.

## Requirements

- The script or brief, duration, platform, and aspect ratio.
- The available crew, locations, and equipment.
- Any brand or legal constraints on claims and music.

## How to use

1. Answer the constraint table.
2. The agent writes the beats and confirms the single takeaway.
3. It produces timed shots and checks the total against the runtime.
4. It groups shots into setups and writes the asset checklist.
5. It flags the riskiest shots with alternatives before the shoot.

## Example

```
Use video-storyboard for a 60s vertical launch video, shot solo on a phone.
```

Result: six beats, 14 shots at 0:58, three setups, an asset list including the music licence, and
two flagged shots with simpler alternatives.

## Limitations

- Cannot judge performance or on-camera presence.
- Timing estimates are planning figures, not edit-locked durations.
- Does not produce actual storyboard drawings unless an image tool is used separately.
- Legal review of claims and music licensing remains the user's responsibility.
