# Film Production Insurance Clearance Audit

## Description
Audits filming locations, stunts, and equipment packages for film production insurance coverage and safety compliance. Use when securing permits and mitigating production liability.

## Purpose
Production insurance protects against actor injuries, damaged camera gear, and location property damage. Insurance audits ensure stunts, pyrotechnics, and drone flights are properly underwritten.

## When to Use
- When executing film production insurance clearance audit in production workflows.
- Use when structured standards, deterministic outputs, and error handling are required.

## When NOT to Use

### Downtown City Street Filming Permit Clearance
Filing a $2,000,000 general liability COI with the municipal film commission to close public sidewalks.
