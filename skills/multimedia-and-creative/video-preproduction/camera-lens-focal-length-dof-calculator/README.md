# Camera Lens Focal Length Dof Calculator

## Description
Calculates optical depth of field, hyperfocal distance, and angle of view for cinema camera sensors and focal lengths. Use when planning shot lists, lens packages, and camera positions.

## Purpose
Knowing exact optical depth of field prevents soft focus on set. Calculating circle of confusion, sensor crop factor, aperture f-stop, and subject distance ensures tack-sharp focus on actors.

## When to Use
- When executing camera lens focal length dof calculator in production workflows.
- Use when structured standards, deterministic outputs, and error handling are required.

## When NOT to Use
- Ad-hoc, unstructured exploratory tasks where formal workflows add unnecessary overhead.
- Environments where required dependencies (Optics Mathematics, Depth of Field (DOF)) cannot be provisioned.

## Supported Technologies
- Optics Mathematics
- Depth of Field (DOF)
- Cinema Sensors
- Camera Planning

## Example Use Cases
### Cinematic Dialogue Focus Pull Planning
Calculating that an 85mm lens at f/1.4 gives only 2 inches of DOF at 5 feet, requiring a dedicated focus puller.

### Studio Green Screen Lens Matching
Matching virtual 3D camera focal length and field of view to physical cinema lenses on set.
