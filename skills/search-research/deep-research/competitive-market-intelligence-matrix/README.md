# Competitive Market Intelligence Matrix

## Description
Constructs structured competitive matrices comparing product features, pricing models, and market positioning. Use when conducting comprehensive market landscape and competitor research.

## Purpose
Strategic market research requires systematic competitor profiling across feature capability, target market segments, pricing tiers, and technological differentiation to spot market white-space.

## When to Use
- When executing competitive market intelligence matrix in production workflows.
- Use when structured standards, deterministic outputs, and error handling are required.

## When NOT to Use
- Ad-hoc, unstructured exploratory tasks where formal workflows add unnecessary overhead.
- Environments where required dependencies (Markdown, Market Research) cannot be provisioned.

## Supported Technologies
- Markdown
- Market Research
- Data Synthesis
- Spreadsheets

## Example Use Cases
### New Product Market Evaluation
Assessing competitive saturation before launching a new SaaS offering.

### Sales Battle-Card Creation
Equipping enterprise sales teams with feature comparison matrices against competitors.

## Usage Instructions
1. Review the operational workflow defined in `SKILL.md`.
2. Ensure runtime dependencies (Markdown) are configured in the host environment.
3. Execute the workflow steps sequentially, validating intermediate outputs.
4. Verify the final generated artifact against declared acceptance criteria.
