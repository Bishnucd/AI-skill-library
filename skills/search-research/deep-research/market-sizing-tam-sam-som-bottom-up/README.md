# Market Sizing Tam Sam Som Bottom Up

## Description
Builds rigorous bottom-up Total Addressable Market (TAM), SAM, and SOM financial models with validated assumptions. Use when evaluating new market entry potential and fundraising models.

## Purpose
Top-down market sizing ("if we get 1% of China") is fundamentally flawed. Rigorous bottom-up sizing multiplies verified customer counts by realistic annual contract values (ACV) grounded in actual sales data.

## When to Use
- When executing market sizing tam sam som bottom up in production workflows.
- Use when structured standards, deterministic outputs, and error handling are required.

## When NOT to Use
- Ad-hoc, unstructured exploratory tasks where formal workflows add unnecessary overhead.
- Environments where required dependencies (TAM / SAM / SOM, Bottom-Up Financial Modeling) cannot be provisioned.

## Supported Technologies
- TAM / SAM / SOM
- Bottom-Up Financial Modeling
- Market Sizing

### Cybersecurity SaaS New Market Entry Sizing
Calculating a $2.4B SAM for cloud identity governance across mid-market healthcare organizations.

### B2B Logistics Marketplace SOM Projection
Modeling realistic 5-year SOM revenue of $45M based on sales rep capacity and carrier onboarding rates.
