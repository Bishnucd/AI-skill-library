# Records Retention Schedule Policy

## Description
Authors legally compliant corporate records retention and disposition schedules across tax, personnel, and customer records. Use when establishing legal retention periods and safe data disposal.

## Purpose
Keeping data too long invites legal discovery risk and storage costs, while discarding it too early violates tax laws. A records retention schedule defines exact statutory retention years and disposal methods.

## When to Use
- When executing records retention schedule policy in production workflows.
- Use when structured standards, deterministic outputs, and error handling are required.

## When NOT to Use
- Ad-hoc, unstructured exploratory tasks where formal workflows add unnecessary overhead.
- Environments where required dependencies (ARMA International, Records Management) cannot be provisioned.

## Supported Technologies
- ARMA International
- Records Management
- Compliance
- Markdown

## Example Use Cases
### Corporate Compliance Policy
Fulfilling IRS and corporate law record-keeping mandates across all business subsidiaries.

### Litigation Discovery Risk Reduction
Routinely destroying obsolete data according to policy to avoid costly legal discovery searches.
