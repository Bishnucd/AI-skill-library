# Facilities Workplace Ergonomic Audit

## Description
Structures workplace physical safety, ergonomic assessments, and environmental health checklists per OSHA standards. Use when auditing physical office spaces and home workstations for employee safety.

## Purpose
Workplace ergonomic injuries cost millions in lost productivity and workers comp claims. Auditing desk height, monitor placement, chair lumbar support, and lighting prevents repetitive strain injuries.

## When to Use
- When executing facilities workplace ergonomic audit in production workflows.
- Use when structured standards, deterministic outputs, and error handling are required.

## When NOT to Use
- Ad-hoc, unstructured exploratory tasks where formal workflows add unnecessary overhead.
- Environments where required dependencies (OSHA Ergonomics Guidelines, Checklists) cannot be provisioned.

## Supported Technologies
- OSHA Ergonomics Guidelines
- Checklists
- Workplace Safety
- Markdown

## Example Use Cases
### Remote Work Ergonomic Review
Helping remote employees configure healthy, injury-free home office setups.

### Office Safety Compliance
Fulfilling occupational health and safety audit requirements across corporate facilities.
