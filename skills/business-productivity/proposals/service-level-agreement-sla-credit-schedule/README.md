# Service Level Agreement Sla Credit Schedule

## Description
Designs contractual Service Level Agreements (SLA) with uptime tiers, response times, and financial credit penalties. Use when drafting commercial contract terms for enterprise SaaS and managed services.

## Purpose
Enterprise customers demand legal SLA commitments. A well-designed SLA defines uptime measurement formulas, exclusions (scheduled maintenance, DDoS), support response times, and rebate credits.

## When to Use
- When executing service level agreement sla credit schedule in production workflows.
- Use when structured standards, deterministic outputs, and error handling are required.

## When NOT to Use
- Ad-hoc, unstructured exploratory tasks where formal workflows add unnecessary overhead.
- Environments where required dependencies (SLA Standards, Contracting) cannot be provisioned.

Drafting standard SLA schedules for enterprise B2B SaaS software contracts.

Establishing clear uptime guarantees and financial credits for cloud hosting services.
