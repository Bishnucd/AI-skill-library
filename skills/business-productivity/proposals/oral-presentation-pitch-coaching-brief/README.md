# Oral Presentation Pitch Coaching Brief

## Description
Structures oral proposal presentation decks, speaker handoffs, and Q&A defense coaching for pitch teams. Use when preparing pursuit teams for competitive shortlist finalist presentations.

## Purpose
Shortlisted competitive bids are won or lost in oral presentations. Preparing clear slide handoffs, time allocations, and rehearsed answers for hostile competitor questions wins the deal.

## When to Use
- When executing oral presentation pitch coaching brief in production workflows.
- Use when structured standards, deterministic outputs, and error handling are required.

## When NOT to Use
- Ad-hoc, unstructured exploratory tasks where formal workflows add unnecessary overhead.
- Environments where required dependencies (Oral Presentations, Speaker Coaching) cannot be provisioned.

## Supported Technologies
- Oral Presentations
- Speaker Coaching
- Pitch Deck
- Markdown

## Example Use Cases
### Enterprise Shortlist Pitch Prep
Coaching an engineering and sales pursuit team before final client board presentations.

### Government Tender Orals
Ensuring defense team stays strictly within allotted time limits during formal evaluation.
