# Software Licensing Commercial Term Sheet

## Description
Drafts commercial term sheets covering software licensing models (per-seat, usage-based, core-based) and payment terms. Use when structuring enterprise software sales agreements.

## Purpose
Term sheets summarize high-level commercial terms before full contract legal review. Clearly defining licensing metrics, payment schedules (Net 30), and renewal price caps avoids closing friction.

## When to Use
- When executing software licensing commercial term sheet in production workflows.
- Use when structured standards, deterministic outputs, and error handling are required.

## When NOT to Use
- Ad-hoc, unstructured exploratory tasks where formal workflows add unnecessary overhead.
- Environments where required dependencies (Software Licensing, Term Sheets) cannot be provisioned.

## Supported Technologies
- Software Licensing
- Term Sheets
- Commercial Pricing
- Markdown

## Example Use Cases
### Enterprise Deal Term Alignment
Securing customer executive sign-off on pricing and license models before legal redlines.

### SaaS Expansion Deal Structuring
Drafting multi-year enterprise volume license agreements with tiered pricing discounts.
